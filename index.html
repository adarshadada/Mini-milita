Jetpack Militia

Strap on a jetpack and win a deathmatch against bots or real players.

Desktop
A/D move, W or Space jetpack, mouse aim and fire, G grenade, R reload, Q swap gun, F fullscreen
Phone
Left stick moves, push up to fly. Right stick aims, push far to fire. Use the side buttons for grenade, swap and reload.

Friends who enter the same room code and game mode play together. Free for all goes to 15 kills, team games to 20 team kills.

Upgrades

0 credits

Earn credits from kills and by finishing matches. Upgrades are saved on this device and apply in every mode.
